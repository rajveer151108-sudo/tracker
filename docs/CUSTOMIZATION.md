# Customization Principles

Afterglow should give listeners control over how their music looks, moves, and feels. Defaults should make the app immediately enjoyable, but defaults must not become restrictions.

## Product rule

Whenever we add an effect or listening feature, consider controls for:

- enable or disable
- intensity, speed, scale, blur, glow, and opacity
- colors from artwork, custom colors, and theme presets
- lyric layout, timing offset, transition style, highlighting, emoji accents, and information density
- background style, motion, screen placement, and display selection
- automatic behavior by genre or song and a manual override
- accessibility, reduced motion, readability, and performance

## Presets and portability

Users should be able to save named presets, switch quickly between them, restore defaults, and eventually export or share settings. Device-specific choices should remain local while account-level preferences may sync across devices.

## Boundaries

Customization must not weaken account security, corrupt timing or play statistics, hide essential safety and account controls, or make the app unusable without a clear reset path.
